Voice assistant

Speak in Telugu or English, translate both ways, and listen to the result.

This browser does not support speech recognition. Chrome or Edge on a desktop works best. You can still type and translate.

1. Speak or type

2. Translation

Your translation will appear here.

3. AI answer

Speak your trip request (for example: "నేను పూణే నుంచి ముంబైకి మూడు రోజులు వెళ్లాలి"), translate it, then send it to the assistant.

How this works

  1. Your speech becomes text in one of two ways. Chrome and Edge do it inside the browser (Web Speech API), which is instant. If your browser cannot, we record the microphone and send the audio to our server, which uses Whisper to write it out.
  2. The text is sent to our Django server.
  3. The server asks the Groq AI to translate it, using the API key stored safely on the server.
  4. The translation comes back and your browser can read it aloud (Speech Synthesis API).

Telugu voices depend on your device. If nothing is spoken for Telugu, your system does not have a Telugu voice installed; the text still appears.
